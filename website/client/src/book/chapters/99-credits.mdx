<h2 id="sec-authors">Authors</h2>

Written by the TVL maintainers at Traigent, with contributions credited in the repository history.

<h2 id="sec-made-with">Made with</h2>

- **Manim Community Edition** (MIT) renders the animations. All animations are original; no material from 3Blue1Brown's videos is used.
- **Mermaid** (MIT) draws the diagrams.
- **Paged.js** (MIT) paginates the printed edition.
- **Playwright** (Apache-2.0) prints the pages to PDF.
- **Source Serif 4**, **Inter** and **JetBrains Mono** (SIL Open Font License 1.1) are the typefaces of the text, self-hosted. Inter and JetBrains Mono also set the labels inside the animations.
