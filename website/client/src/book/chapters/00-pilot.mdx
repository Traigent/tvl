<h2 id="sec-learn">What you'll learn</h2>

This chapter is a rendering test, not teaching material. It carries every
component the book needs, so a bad page break or a clipped line shows up before
any real chapter is written. The words are placeholders.

By the end you should see a long listing, a table, a diagram, an explorable
with a print fallback, an animation shown as a frame sequence, and the boxes
the real chapters use. Look at the <Term id="design-space">design space</Term>
figure in <XRef to="sec-figure">the figure section</XRef>, then read
<Ref to="rfc-0003" section="section 3.2">the monitor requirements in RFC 0003</Ref>
for the authoritative wording.

<h2 id="sec-table">A table</h2>

| Predicate kind | What it asks | Example in plain words | Where it is defined |
|---|---|---|---|
| Absence | Something must never happen | No refund above the limit is approved | Formal semantics, section 12.3 |
| Bounded existence | Something may happen at most N times | At most three tool calls per task | RFC 0003, section 6.5 |
| Precedence | One event must come before another | A check runs before every payment | RFC 0003, section 6.5 |
| Bounded response | A reply must follow within a window | Every escalation gets an answer in ten minutes | RFC 0003, section 6.5 |
| Metric threshold | A unit's metric must not exceed a limit | No answer takes longer than thirty seconds | RFC 0003, section 6.5 |
