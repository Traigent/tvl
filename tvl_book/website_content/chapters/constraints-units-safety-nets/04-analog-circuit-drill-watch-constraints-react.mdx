The lab shows two kinds of warning. Only the rerank rule is a structural constraint (a rule over tuned variables, or TVARs);
the latency and cost warnings are thresholds on simulated outcomes, the kind of target a real module
states as an objective or a measured requirement:

- **Latency under 800 ms** (simulated outcome) — raises amber/red when the simulated configuration breaches the ceiling.
- **Cost under $0.12** (simulated outcome) — highlights the response-token capacitor if the API budget slips.
- **Rerank weight ≥ 0.3** (structural) — conditional guard for `retriever_top_k >= 40` to prevent noisy answers.

Challenge yourself to bring all three back to green without dropping quality below 75.
